# Restaurant Prompt

**Purpose:** Bootstrap a restaurant website under the full CEF standard — menu, atmosphere, and reservations.

**Description:** This prompt kicks off a restaurant or hospitality site where mood and information both matter. It drives discovery to capture the cuisine, brand atmosphere, and conversion goal (reservations, orders, visits), selects the `landing-page` or `nextjs` template, and applies the design, images, copywriting, SEO, AI-SEO (local), performance, and accessibility standards. The output target is an appetizing, trustworthy site that turns browsers into diners.

## What this prompt will produce

- A menu, atmosphere gallery, hours/location, and reservation/ordering path.
- Local SEO and structured data (restaurant, menu, hours).
- Fast, image-rich pages that still meet performance budgets.

## Archetype notes

- Atmosphere sells; imagery and copy set the mood.
- Local discovery (maps, hours, reservations) is critical.
- Menus must be accessible and easy to update.
