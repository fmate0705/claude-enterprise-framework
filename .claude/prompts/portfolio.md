# Portfolio Prompt

**Purpose:** Bootstrap a personal or studio portfolio under the full CEF standard — work-forward and memorable.

**Description:** This prompt kicks off a portfolio site for an individual or studio, where the work is the hero. It drives discovery to capture the body of work, personal brand, and desired impression, selects the `landing-page` or `nextjs` template, and applies the design, motion, images, copywriting, performance, and accessibility standards. The output target is a distinctive, tasteful showcase that converts viewers into inquiries.

## What this prompt will produce

- A work-forward showcase with case studies or project galleries.
- Distinctive but disciplined design and motion.
- A clear path to contact or hire.

## Archetype notes

- The work leads; chrome recedes.
- Room for personality and craft within CEF's taste guardrails.
- Image performance and motion restraint keep it premium, not gaudy.
