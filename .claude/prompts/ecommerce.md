# Ecommerce Prompt

**Purpose:** Bootstrap an online store under the full CEF standard — catalog, product, cart, and checkout.

**Description:** This prompt kicks off an ecommerce build. It drives discovery to capture the catalog, brand, and fulfillment model, selects the `fullstack` or `nextjs` template, and applies the architecture, performance, images, SEO, AI-SEO, security, accessibility, and legal standards. The output target is a fast, trustworthy storefront where browsing is delightful and checkout is frictionless.

## What this prompt will produce

- Catalog and product pages optimized for performance and SEO.
- Cart and checkout flows built with security and accessibility in mind.
- Compliant policy pages (returns, privacy, terms) per the legal standard.

## Archetype notes

- Image performance and product-page speed drive conversion.
- Checkout security and trust signals are non-negotiable.
- Structured data (products, reviews) is essential for discovery.
