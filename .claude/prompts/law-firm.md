# Law Firm Prompt

**Purpose:** Bootstrap a law firm website under the full CEF standard — authoritative, credible, and conversion-aware.

**Description:** This prompt kicks off a legal practice site where authority and trust drive engagement. It drives discovery to capture practice areas, attorneys, and jurisdictions, selects the `company` or `nextjs` template, and applies the design, copywriting, SEO, AI-SEO (local), accessibility, and legal standards. The output target is a polished, credible firm site that communicates expertise and makes it easy to request a consultation.

## What this prompt will produce

- Practice areas, attorney profiles, results/testimonials, and consultation paths.
- Strong local and topical SEO for legal queries.
- Compliant disclaimers and policy pages per the legal standard.

## Archetype notes

- Authority and precision in tone and design.
- Practice-area and attorney IA supports SEO and trust.
- Required legal disclaimers must be present and correct.
