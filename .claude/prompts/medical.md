# Medical Prompt

**Purpose:** Bootstrap a medical or healthcare practice website under the full CEF standard — trustworthy, accessible, and compliant.

**Description:** This prompt kicks off a healthcare site (clinic, practice, or provider) where trust, accessibility, and compliance are paramount. It drives discovery to capture services, providers, and regulatory context, selects the `nextjs` or `company` template, and applies the design, copywriting, accessibility, security, SEO, AI-SEO (local), and legal standards with extra rigor. The output target is a calm, credible, fully accessible site that helps patients and respects compliance.

## What this prompt will produce

- Services, providers, and appointment/contact paths.
- Elevated accessibility (many users have access needs).
- Compliance-aware handling of forms and patient data per the legal and security standards.

## Archetype notes

- Trust and calm over marketing flash.
- Accessibility is elevated, not just met.
- Any patient-data handling triggers heightened security and legal review.
