# Company Website Prompt

**Purpose:** Bootstrap a professional multi-page company / corporate website under the full CEF standard.

**Description:** This prompt kicks off a corporate presence: home, about, services/products, and contact, with room for careers, news, and case studies. It drives discovery to capture the company's positioning and brand, selects an appropriate template (`nextjs` or `agency`), and applies the design, copywriting, SEO, AI-SEO, performance, accessibility, and legal standards. The output target is a credible, well-structured site that builds trust.

## What this prompt will produce

- A multi-page company site with clear information architecture.
- Trust-building content: about, services, proof, contact.
- Compliant policy pages (privacy, terms) per the legal standard.

## Archetype notes

- Credibility and clarity over flash.
- Strong SEO/AI-SEO foundation for organic discovery.
- Scalable IA that can grow with the business.
