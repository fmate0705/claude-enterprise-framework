# Hotel Prompt

**Purpose:** Bootstrap a hotel or hospitality website under the full CEF standard — immersive, informative, and booking-driven.

**Description:** This prompt kicks off a hotel, resort, or lodging site where atmosphere and booking conversion both matter. It drives discovery to capture the property, brand experience, and booking model, selects the `landing-page` or `nextjs` template, and applies the design, motion, images, copywriting, SEO, AI-SEO (local), performance, and accessibility standards. The output target is an immersive site that conveys the experience and drives direct bookings.

## What this prompt will produce

- Rooms, amenities, gallery, location, and a booking/inquiry path.
- Immersive imagery balanced against performance budgets.
- Local and travel-oriented SEO with structured data (hotel, rooms, amenities).

## Archetype notes

- Sell the experience through imagery, motion, and copy — within performance limits.
- Direct booking conversion is the primary goal.
- Local and travel discovery (maps, amenities, reviews) matter.
