# Design Standard

**Purpose:** Establish the visual design language of CEF — the principles, tokens, and taste that make CEF-built interfaces look intentional, premium, and coherent.

**Description:** This standard defines how CEF thinks about visual design: spacing scales, typographic rhythm, color systems, elevation, radius, and the design tokens that encode them. It is opinionated about restraint — generous whitespace, a tight type scale, and a limited, deliberate palette. It will articulate the difference between a design that merely functions and one that feels designed, and give Claude concrete rules to reach the latter every time.

## Scope

- Design tokens: spacing, typography, color, radius, shadow, z-index.
- Layout principles: grid, whitespace, hierarchy, and rhythm.
- Design taste: what "premium" means in measurable terms.
- Theming (light/dark) and token architecture.
- The relationship between design, UI, and motion standards.

## Status

Draft (AS-000). This standard is scaffolded; its rules will be authored in a later module.
