# Legal Standard

**Purpose:** Define the legal and compliance baseline for CEF sites — privacy, cookies, consent, accessibility law, and required policy documents.

**Description:** Shipping a public site carries legal obligations. This standard will prescribe the compliance defaults CEF applies: privacy policy and terms coverage, cookie consent and tracking disclosure, GDPR/CCPA data-handling posture, accessibility-law alignment (ADA/EAA), and the placement of required notices. It is not legal advice, but it ensures CEF sites start from a defensible, standards-aware baseline rather than ignoring compliance until a complaint arrives.

## Scope

- Required policy documents (privacy, terms, cookies).
- Consent management and tracking disclosure.
- Data-protection posture (GDPR, CCPA) and data minimization.
- Accessibility-law alignment (ADA, EAA).
- Attribution, licensing, and third-party content compliance.

## Status

Draft (AS-000). This standard is scaffolded; its rules will be authored in a later module.
