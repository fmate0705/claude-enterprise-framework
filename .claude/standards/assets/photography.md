# Photography

**Framework:** CEF · **Specification:** AS-012 (Brand & Asset Intelligence Engine) · **Version:** 0.1.0

**Purpose:** Fix photographic style and usage. Photography MUST be authentic, consistent, and on-direction. Stock clichés are Never used.

**Language:** MUST / MUST NOT / SHOULD / MAY per RFC 2119.

---

## Photography Rules

- **PH-01 — Authenticity.** Photography MUST be authentic and relevant (real product, team, or context); generic stock clichés (handshakes, generic offices) MUST NOT be used (`BP-09`, `experience/trust.md`).
- **PH-02 — Consistent composition.** Framing and composition conventions MUST be consistent across a set; a jarring mix MUST NOT be used.
- **PH-03 — Consistent lighting.** Lighting quality and direction MUST be consistent with the art direction; mismatched lighting MUST NOT be used (`art-direction.md` AD-03).
- **PH-04 — Depth of field.** Depth-of-field conventions MUST be consistent; arbitrary focus that fights the subject MUST NOT be used.
- **PH-05 — Subject framing.** The subject MUST be framed clearly with intentional negative space; cluttered or ambiguous framing MUST NOT be used.
- **PH-06 — Emotion and relevance.** Photography SHOULD convey the intended, honest emotion and relate to the content; irrelevant mood shots MUST NOT be used.
- **PH-07 — Color treatment.** Photography MUST be color-graded to the brand grade consistently (`color-system.md` CLR-04).
- **PH-08 — Avoid clichés.** Overused stock clichés and posed inauthenticity MUST NOT be used.
- **PH-09 — Real people, real permission.** Photos of real people MUST be used with permission and represent the brand honestly; misrepresenting stock people as customers/staff MUST NOT be done (`content/trust-signals.md`).
- **PH-10 — Optimized and responsive.** Photography MUST be optimized, correctly sized, and served responsively (`image-optimization.md`, `responsive-images.md`).
- **PH-11 — Accessible.** Content photos MUST have descriptive `alt`; decorative photos MUST use empty `alt` (`image-seo.md`).

## When Photography Is Unavailable

- **PH-12 — Generate consistently.** When suitable photography is unavailable, consistent imagery MUST be generated via the Higgsfield MCP to the art direction (`art-direction.md` AD-08…14); random stock MUST NOT be substituted for a required brand asset (Article IV).
- **PH-13 — Honest generation.** Generated imagery MUST NOT misrepresent reality (fake people presented as real customers, fake premises); such use MUST NOT occur (`content/trust-signals.md`).

## Photography Guarantees

- **PH-G1** — Authentic, consistent, on-direction photography.
- **PH-G2** — Consistent lighting, grade, and framing; no clichés.
- **PH-G3** — Generated to direction when unavailable; never misrepresenting reality.
