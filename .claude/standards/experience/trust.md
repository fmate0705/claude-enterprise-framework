# Visual Trust Signals

**Framework:** CEF · **Specification:** AS-008 (Experience Engine) · **Version:** 0.1.0

**Purpose:** Fix how design earns credibility. Trust is the objective of premium design. Every signal MUST be real; a fabricated signal destroys trust the moment it is noticed.

**Language:** MUST / MUST NOT / SHOULD / MAY per RFC 2119.

---

## Trust Rules

- **TR-01 — Authentic imagery.** Imagery MUST be real and on-brand (product, team, work); generic stock clichés (handshakes, generic offices) MUST NOT be used.
- **TR-02 — Consistent branding.** A consistent identity across type, color, and detail MUST be maintained; inconsistency reads as carelessness and MUST NOT be shipped (`branding.md`).
- **TR-03 — Readable copy.** Copy MUST be legible and specific; walls of text, vague claims, and jargon MUST NOT be used (`copy-hierarchy.md`).
- **TR-04 — Authentic content.** Testimonials, reviews, logos, and case studies MUST be real and attributed; fabricated proof MUST NOT be used (Constitution Article IV).
- **TR-05 — Real numbers.** Statistics and metrics MUST be true and verifiable; invented numbers MUST NOT be presented as fact.
- **TR-06 — Legal transparency.** Required legal (privacy, terms, cookie disclosure, pricing) MUST be present and honest; hidden terms and costs MUST NOT be used.
- **TR-07 — Performance as trust.** The interface MUST be fast within budget; slowness reads as low quality and erodes trust (`principles.md` XP-P28).
- **TR-08 — Accessibility as trust.** The interface MUST be accessible; exclusion signals carelessness and erodes credibility (`accessibility.md`).
- **TR-09 — Working details.** Links MUST work, states MUST be handled, and errors MUST be graceful; broken details destroy trust disproportionately.
- **TR-10 — Honest urgency and claims.** Urgency, scarcity, and claims MUST be truthful; fake urgency and overpromises MUST NOT be used (`cta.md` CTA-09).

## How Design Influences Credibility

Credibility is cumulative (`premium.md` PRM-02): many correct details build it, and a few wrong ones destroy it. A single fabricated testimonial, broken link, or low-contrast heading can outweigh an otherwise polished page. Trust is therefore protected by correctness and honesty, verified at review (`review.md`).

## Trust Guarantees

- **TR-G1** — Every trust signal is real, attributed, and verifiable.
- **TR-G2** — Consistent branding, legible copy, working details.
- **TR-G3** — Fast, accessible, legally transparent, and honest.
