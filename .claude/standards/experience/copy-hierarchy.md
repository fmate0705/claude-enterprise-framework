# Content Hierarchy

**Framework:** CEF · **Specification:** AS-008 (Experience Engine) · **Version:** 0.1.0

**Purpose:** Fix how written content is structured for scanning and comprehension. Content comes first; the layout serves the copy.

**Language:** MUST / MUST NOT / SHOULD / MAY per RFC 2119.

---

## Content Hierarchy Rules

- **CH-01 — Message first.** Each section MUST lead with its single most important message; supporting detail MUST follow.
- **CH-02 — Benefit-led headlines.** Headlines MUST state a benefit or a clear point; vague or generic headlines ("We deliver excellence") MUST NOT be used.
- **CH-03 — Scannable structure.** Content MUST be scannable — short paragraphs, meaningful subheads, lists where appropriate; walls of text MUST NOT be used.
- **CH-04 — One idea per unit.** Each paragraph or block MUST carry one idea; multiple ideas MUST NOT be crammed into one block.
- **CH-05 — Front-load answers.** Answers and key facts MUST be front-loaded under clear headings so they are found quickly (also aids AI-SEO).
- **CH-06 — Descriptive labels.** Links, buttons, and headings MUST be descriptive; "click here" and non-descriptive labels MUST NOT be used.
- **CH-07 — Progressive detail.** Detail MUST be layered — summary first, depth on demand (`principles.md` XP-P27).
- **CH-08 — Real, specific content.** Content MUST be real and specific; placeholder, filler, and vague claims MUST NOT be used (`principles.md` XP-P29).
- **CH-09 — Consistent voice.** Copy MUST follow the brand voice consistently (`branding.md` BR-03).
- **CH-10 — Measured reading.** Long-form content MUST use the reading measure (45–75ch) and reading density profile (`typography.md`, `content-density.md`).

## Content-Hierarchy Guarantees

- **CH-G1** — Each section leads with one clear, benefit-led message.
- **CH-G2** — Content is scannable, specific, and real.
- **CH-G3** — Answers are front-loaded; detail is progressive.
