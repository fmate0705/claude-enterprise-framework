# Copywriting Standard

**Purpose:** Define the voice, structure, and quality of the words in CEF-built products — headlines, body copy, microcopy, and calls to action.

**Description:** Words are interface. This standard will prescribe how CEF writes: clear over clever, specific over vague, active over passive, and benefit-led over feature-led. It will cover headline and subhead structure, landing-page narrative arc, microcopy for buttons, forms, and errors, tone calibration by industry, and the ban on filler and lorem ipsum. It coordinates with the design and UI standards so copy and layout are designed together, not bolted on.

## Scope

- Voice and tone principles; calibration by audience/industry.
- Headline, subhead, and section-copy structure.
- Landing-page narrative arc and messaging hierarchy.
- Microcopy: buttons, forms, empty states, errors.
- The no-filler, no-lorem-ipsum rule.
