# Content Anti-Patterns

**Framework:** CEF · **Specification:** AS-011 (Content Intelligence Engine) · **Version:** 0.1.0

**Purpose:** Document the content anti-patterns this engine forbids. Each states the Problem, its Impact, and the Preferred alternative. Content containing any of these MUST be corrected before it passes review. This catalog operationalizes Constitution Article IV for written content. (Required by the AS-011 anti-patterns mandate; it is not in the module's file list but is authored here as its canonical home.)

**Enforcement:** When a listed anti-pattern is detected, the content MUST NOT pass `review.md`/`validation.md`. The Preferred alternative MUST be applied.

**Language:** MUST / MUST NOT / SHOULD / MAY per RFC 2119.

---

## Anti-Pattern Guarantees

- **CNAP-G1** — Detection of any listed anti-pattern MUST fail content review.
- **CNAP-G2** — The Preferred alternative is the required fix; a cosmetic fix MUST NOT pass.
- **CNAP-G3** — Placeholder, fabrication, hype, and dark patterns are never permitted (Constitution Article IV).
