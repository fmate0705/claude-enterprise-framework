# About Pages

**Framework:** CEF · **Specification:** AS-011 (Content Intelligence Engine) · **Version:** 0.1.0

**Purpose:** Fix how About pages are written. An About page MUST build trust through an honest story, real people, and clear values.

**Language:** MUST / MUST NOT / SHOULD / MAY per RFC 2119.

---

## About Page Rules

- **ABT-01 — Structure.** An About page MUST cover: mission/story → values → team → proof → contact/CTA (`page-structure.md` PST-10).
- **ABT-02 — Honest story.** The company story MUST be true; invented origin stories or milestones MUST NOT be published (`CNP-16`).
- **ABT-03 — Real people.** Team members MUST be real, with real names, roles, and (where used) real photos; fabricated team members MUST NOT be shown.
- **ABT-04 — Clear mission and values.** The mission and values MUST be stated plainly and specifically; generic value clichés ("integrity, excellence") MUST NOT stand alone without meaning.
- **ABT-05 — Reader relevance.** The About page MUST connect the story to reader value (why it matters to them), not only self-description (`CNP-38`).
- **ABT-06 — Real proof.** Credibility elements (history, scale, clients) MUST be real and verifiable (`trust-signals.md`).
- **ABT-07 — Human tone.** The tone MUST be authentic and human, not corporate boilerplate (`CNP-05`).
- **ABT-08 — Contact path.** The page MUST provide a next step (contact, careers, or primary CTA).
- **ABT-09 — Legal identity.** Where required by jurisdiction, company legal identity MUST be present or linked (`legal-pages.md`, Impressum/Impresszum).
- **ABT-10 — Maintained.** Team, milestones, and figures MUST be kept current (`CNP-40`).

## About Page Guarantees

- **ABT-G1** — Honest story, real people, clear mission and values.
- **ABT-G2** — Reader-relevant, human, with real proof.
- **ABT-G3** — Legal identity where required; maintained and current.
