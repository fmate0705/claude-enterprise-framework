# Content Philosophy

**Framework:** CEF · **Specification:** AS-011 (Content Intelligence Engine) · **Version:** 0.1.0

**Purpose:** State the principles that govern every content decision. Each states its Purpose, Reasoning, a Good example (✓), and a Bad example (✗). The principles are binding and prevent generic AI writing.

**Language:** MUST / MUST NOT / SHOULD / MAY per RFC 2119.

---

### CNP-01 — People Read to Solve Problems
- **Purpose:** Serve the reader's task. **Reasoning:** Visitors arrive with a goal, not to admire prose.
- ✓ "Deploy in one command. No config." ✗ "Welcome to our innovative platform."

### CNP-02 — Good Content Removes Uncertainty
- **Purpose:** Answer the reader's open questions. **Reasoning:** Uncertainty stalls decisions.
- ✓ "Free for up to 3 users. $12/user after." ✗ "Flexible pricing to suit your needs."

### CNP-03 — Clear Language Builds Trust
- **Purpose:** Write plainly. **Reasoning:** Clarity signals competence; obscurity signals evasion.
- ✓ "We store your data in the EU." ✗ "We leverage best-in-class data residency paradigms."

### CNP-04 — Specificity Beats Generic Statements
- **Purpose:** Use concrete detail. **Reasoning:** Specifics are believable and memorable.
- ✓ "Cuts onboarding from 3 weeks to 2 days." ✗ "Dramatically improves efficiency."

### CNP-05 — Authenticity Beats Marketing Jargon
- **Purpose:** Sound human. **Reasoning:** Jargon reads as hollow and interchangeable.
- ✓ "We help small clinics book more patients." ✗ "Synergizing holistic healthcare solutions."

### CNP-06 — Lead With the Benefit
- **Purpose:** State the value first. **Reasoning:** Readers decide in seconds whether to continue.
- ✓ "Get paid 5 days faster." ✗ "Our invoicing module supports multiple workflows."

### CNP-07 — Front-Load the Point
- **Purpose:** Put the answer first. **Reasoning:** Readers scan; buried points are missed.
- ✓ "Yes — cancel anytime. Here's how." ✗ A paragraph that reaches the answer at the end.

### CNP-08 — Write for the Reader, Not the Brand
- **Purpose:** Use "you" framing. **Reasoning:** Content is about the reader's outcome.
- ✓ "You'll ship faster." ✗ "We are passionate about shipping."

### CNP-09 — Concrete Over Abstract
- **Purpose:** Prefer tangible nouns and verbs. **Reasoning:** Abstractions are hard to picture and easy to doubt.
- ✓ "Scan a receipt; we file the expense." ✗ "Streamline your financial operations."

### CNP-10 — Show, Don't Claim
- **Purpose:** Prove with evidence, not adjectives. **Reasoning:** Proof persuades; adjectives are free.
- ✓ "4,200 teams use it daily." ✗ "The world's most loved tool."

### CNP-11 — Plain Language Over Jargon
- **Purpose:** Use words the reader knows. **Reasoning:** Undefined jargon excludes and confuses.
- ✓ "Works offline." ✗ "Offline-first eventual-consistency architecture."

### CNP-12 — Active Voice Over Passive
- **Purpose:** Name the actor. **Reasoning:** Active voice is shorter, clearer, and more direct.
- ✓ "We review every request." ✗ "Every request is reviewed."

### CNP-13 — Short Sentences Carry Weight
- **Purpose:** Keep sentences tight. **Reasoning:** Long sentences lose the reader.
- ✓ "It's fast. It's simple. It works." ✗ A 40-word sentence with three clauses.

### CNP-14 — One Idea Per Paragraph
- **Purpose:** Separate ideas. **Reasoning:** Mixed ideas are hard to scan and recall.
- ✓ A paragraph per point. ✗ A wall covering five topics at once.

### CNP-15 — Cut Every Unnecessary Word
- **Purpose:** Edit ruthlessly. **Reasoning:** Every extra word dilutes the message.
- ✓ "Start now." ✗ "In order to get started, simply begin the process."

### CNP-16 — Honest Over Persuasive
- **Purpose:** Never mislead. **Reasoning:** A trick noticed destroys trust permanently.
- ✓ "Setup takes about an hour." ✗ "Set up in seconds!" (when it does not).

### CNP-17 — Answer the Real Question
- **Purpose:** Address what the reader actually asks. **Reasoning:** Evasive copy erodes confidence.
- ✓ "Yes, it integrates with Xero." ✗ "We support a wide ecosystem of integrations."

### CNP-18 — Scannable Structure
- **Purpose:** Make copy skimmable. **Reasoning:** Most readers scan before reading.
- ✓ Headings, short paragraphs, lists. ✗ An unbroken block of text.

### CNP-19 — Consistent Terminology
- **Purpose:** Name each thing one way. **Reasoning:** Synonyms for the same feature confuse readers and search.
- ✓ Always "workspace." ✗ "workspace" / "project" / "board" interchangeably.

### CNP-20 — Voice Is Consistent; Tone Adapts
- **Purpose:** Keep one voice; adjust tone to context. **Reasoning:** Consistency builds identity; tone fits the moment.
- ✓ Same voice, gentler tone in an error. ✗ A different personality on every page.

### CNP-21 — Real Content Over Placeholder
- **Purpose:** Never ship lorem ipsum. **Reasoning:** Placeholder is the clearest sign of unfinished work.
- ✓ Request real copy if missing. ✗ "Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet."

### CNP-22 — Numbers Must Be True
- **Purpose:** Only publish verifiable figures. **Reasoning:** One fabricated number discredits all of them.
- ✓ "Serving 1,200 customers." (real) ✗ "10,000+ happy users!" (invented).

### CNP-23 — Write the Way People Speak
- **Purpose:** Use natural language. **Reasoning:** Stiff, robotic copy reads as machine-made.
- ✓ "Not sure which plan? Start free." ✗ "Utilize our complimentary tier to ascertain suitability."

### CNP-24 — Avoid Hype and Superlatives
- **Purpose:** Drop empty intensifiers. **Reasoning:** "Revolutionary" and "best" are unproven and ignored.
- ✓ "Faster than manual entry." ✗ "The most revolutionary solution ever."

### CNP-25 — Respect the Reader's Time
- **Purpose:** Be brief. **Reasoning:** Length without value is disrespect.
- ✓ Three sentences that suffice. ✗ Three paragraphs saying the same thing.

### CNP-26 — Every Word Earns Its Place
- **Purpose:** No filler. **Reasoning:** Filler signals carelessness.
- ✓ Purposeful copy. ✗ "In today's fast-paced world…"

### CNP-27 — Clarity Over Cleverness
- **Purpose:** Prefer clear to witty. **Reasoning:** A pun that obscures the point fails.
- ✓ "Track your spending." ✗ A clever headline no one understands.

### CNP-28 — Guide the Next Action
- **Purpose:** Tell the reader what to do. **Reasoning:** Copy without direction wastes intent.
- ✓ "Compare plans →" ✗ A page that ends with no next step.

### CNP-29 — Address Objections Directly
- **Purpose:** Answer doubts. **Reasoning:** Unaddressed objections stop conversion.
- ✓ "No credit card required." ✗ Ignoring the obvious "what does it cost?"

### CNP-30 — Accessibility Is Readability
- **Purpose:** Write for every reader. **Reasoning:** Plain, structured copy serves everyone, including assistive tech.
- ✓ Descriptive links, plain terms. ✗ "Click here"; dense jargon.

### CNP-31 — SEO Serves Readers, Not Engines
- **Purpose:** Write for people first. **Reasoning:** Reader-first content is what engines reward.
- ✓ Natural, useful copy with relevant terms. ✗ Keyword-stuffed sentences.

### CNP-32 — AI-Readable Is Human-Readable
- **Purpose:** Structure for extraction. **Reasoning:** Clear, factual, well-headed content serves humans and machines alike.
- ✓ Question-headed sections with direct answers. ✗ Vague headings over rambling prose.

### CNP-33 — Localize Meaning, Not Words
- **Purpose:** Adapt intent, not literal text. **Reasoning:** Literal translation loses tone and sense.
- ✓ A localized idiom that lands. ✗ A word-for-word translation that reads oddly.

### CNP-34 — Legal Clarity Protects Users
- **Purpose:** Make required terms clear and findable. **Reasoning:** Hidden or vague terms harm users and trust.
- ✓ Plain-language privacy summary + full policy. ✗ Buried, impenetrable legal text.

### CNP-35 — Edit Ruthlessly
- **Purpose:** Revise before shipping. **Reasoning:** First drafts are rarely tight; editing is where quality is made.
- ✓ Cut 30% on the second pass. ✗ Publishing the first draft.

### CNP-36 — Match Content to Intent
- **Purpose:** Fit copy to the page's purpose and the visitor's stage. **Reasoning:** Wrong-intent copy misses the reader.
- ✓ Comparison content on a comparison page. ✗ A hard sell on a support article.

### CNP-37 — Credibility Compounds
- **Purpose:** Accumulate trust signals honestly. **Reasoning:** Consistent accuracy builds authority over time.
- ✓ Real proof, working links, correct facts. ✗ One fake testimonial undermining the rest.

### CNP-38 — Empathy Over Ego
- **Purpose:** Center the reader's needs, not the brand's pride. **Reasoning:** Self-focused copy repels.
- ✓ "Here's how this helps you." ✗ "We are proud to be industry leaders."

### CNP-39 — Consistency Across the Product
- **Purpose:** One voice and terminology everywhere. **Reasoning:** Inconsistency reads as multiple authors and carelessness.
- ✓ The same terms and voice site-wide. ✗ Marketing and app copy that clash.

### CNP-40 — Content Is a Product, Maintained
- **Purpose:** Keep content current. **Reasoning:** Stale content misleads and decays trust.
- ✓ Updated dates, prices, and facts. ✗ A 2019 pricing page still live.

## Philosophy Guarantees

- **CNP-G1** — Content is clear, specific, honest, and reader-first.
- **CNP-G2** — No placeholder, hype, jargon, or fabricated facts.
- **CNP-G3** — One consistent voice; tone adapts; content is maintained.
