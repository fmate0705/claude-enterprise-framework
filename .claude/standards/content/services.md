# Service Pages

**Framework:** CEF · **Specification:** AS-011 (Content Intelligence Engine) · **Version:** 0.1.0

**Purpose:** Fix how service pages are written. A service page MUST explain the service concretely and convert honestly.

**Language:** MUST / MUST NOT / SHOULD / MAY per RFC 2119.

---

## Service Page Rules

- **SVC-01 — Structure.** A service page MUST cover: what it is → who it's for → benefits → process → proof → pricing/CTA (`page-structure.md` PST-11).
- **SVC-02 — Concrete scope.** The service's scope MUST be described concretely (what is and isn't included); vague "full-service solutions" MUST NOT be used (`CNP-04`).
- **SVC-03 — Audience clarity.** The page MUST state who the service is for; a service for everyone reads as a service for no one.
- **SVC-04 — Benefits over deliverables.** Copy MUST translate deliverables into client benefits (`CNP-06`).
- **SVC-05 — Process transparency.** The process/steps MUST be clear so the client knows what to expect; a mysterious process MUST NOT be used.
- **SVC-06 — Real proof.** Claims MUST be supported by real proof (case studies, testimonials, metrics) (`trust-signals.md`).
- **SVC-07 — Honest pricing.** Pricing or a pricing path MUST be provided honestly (fixed, range, or "request a quote"); hidden or misleading pricing MUST NOT be used.
- **SVC-08 — Clear CTA.** Exactly one primary CTA MUST guide the next step (`calls-to-action.md`).
- **SVC-09 — Objection handling.** Common objections (cost, timeline, fit) SHOULD be addressed.
- **SVC-10 — Consistent naming.** Service names MUST be consistent across the site (`CNP-19`).

## Service Page Guarantees

- **SVC-G1** — Concrete scope, clear audience, benefit-led copy.
- **SVC-G2** — Transparent process and pricing with real proof.
- **SVC-G3** — One clear CTA; objections handled.
