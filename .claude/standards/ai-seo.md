# AI-SEO Standard

**Purpose:** Define how CEF makes sites discoverable and citable by AI systems — LLM answer engines, AI assistants, and generative search.

**Description:** Search is shifting from links to answers. This standard addresses *Generative Engine Optimization*: how to structure content so that AI assistants and answer engines can understand, quote, and cite a site accurately. It will prescribe machine-readable content structure, clear factual statements, entity clarity, `llms.txt`-style manifests, citation-friendly formatting, and content that survives extraction and summarization. It is the forward-looking complement to the traditional SEO standard.

## Scope

- Content structuring for extraction and citation.
- Entity and fact clarity for LLM comprehension.
- `llms.txt` and AI-crawler manifests.
- Answer-engine metadata and Q&A structuring.
- Measuring visibility in AI-mediated discovery.

## Status

**Superseded by the Discoverability Engine (AS-010).** The canonical AI-SEO standard now lives in [`discoverability/`](discoverability/) — see `ai-discoverability.md`, `llms.md`, and `entity-seo.md` for AI discoverability decisions.
