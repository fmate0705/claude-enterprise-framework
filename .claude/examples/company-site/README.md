# Example — Company Site

**Purpose:** Serve as a reference implementation of a multi-page company website built end-to-end to CEF standards.

**Description:** This example will be a complete corporate site demonstrating how CEF handles multi-page information architecture, trust-building content, SEO/AI-SEO foundations, and legal compliance. It shows the `company`/`nextjs` archetype taken to a credible, finished result that a real business could ship.

## What this example will demonstrate

- Scalable information architecture across multiple pages.
- Trust-building content and copy per the copywriting standard.
- Compliant policy pages per the legal standard.
- Passing SEO, performance, and accessibility checklists.
