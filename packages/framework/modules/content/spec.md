# Content — Detailed Specification

Model content explicitly rather than embedding it in components. Content moves through an
editorial workflow with states and transitions; review is required before publish and the
author is never the approver. History is append-only — no role deletes a revision, because
the ability to delete history is the ability to erase accountability. A missed scheduled
publish must surface loudly. Localization formats defer to the localization policy.
Authoritative source: AS-018 (Content Operations).
