# Legal — Detailed Specification

Ship the legal pages the jurisdiction requires — Privacy Policy, Terms, Cookie Policy, and
an Impressum where applicable. For Hungary, use the Hungarian documents (Adatkezelési
Tájékoztató, ÁSZF, Cookie Tájékoztató, Impresszum). Every generated legal document is a
**draft** and MUST carry a prominent disclaimer that it requires review by a qualified legal
professional before publication. Never fabricate legal facts. Authoritative source: AS-000
(Legal), AS-018 (Content Operations — localization).
