# Motion — Detailed Specification

Motion is admitted only when it communicates state, hierarchy, or continuity, and only when
it stays within the performance budget. Keep durations at or under 300ms with standard
easing. A dropdown may fade and scale in; a purely decorative background loop is rejected.
Always honor `prefers-reduced-motion` by reducing or removing non-essential motion.
Authoritative source: AS-011 (Motion).
